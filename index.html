Long before roads carved through these hills, Dak Bungalows stood as quiet waypoints for weary travellers — places of warmth, fire, and simple grace. The Forest Dak Bungalow carries that same spirit into the present.

Nestled among ancient oaks and rhododendrons at Malla Ramgarh, this is your family's private forest home — three bedrooms, a dining table for stories, a kitchen for late-night chai, and a verandah that looks out forever.

Experiences

Days Without Clocks,
Nights Without Plans

There is no itinerary here — only gentle rhythms shaped by the forest, the weather, and whatever your family feels like doing.

🥾

Forest Walks

Ancient trails wind through Ramgarh's oak and rhododendron forests. Walk at sunrise and find the valley still wrapped in mist.

🦜

Birding

Ramgarh is a birder's paradise — Himalayan laughing thrush, kalij pheasant, and if you're quiet enough, a Monal pheasant.

🫖

Kitchen Mornings

No room service here. Just you, a fully stocked kitchen, and the pleasure of cooking breakfast while the forest wakes up.

✨

Stargazing

Explore Ramgarh

A Village That Exists
Outside of Time

Malla Ramgarh sits at 1,789 metres in the Kumaon Himalayas — a village of orchards, forests, and forgotten paths. Known as the fruit bowl of Kumaon, it was a favourite retreat of the poet Rabindranath Tagore.

Ramgarh Village

Colonial bungalows, orchards, and the famous Tagore writing cottage — a 10-minute walk away.

10 min · Walk

Mukteshwar Temple

A sacred Shiva temple at 2,286m with views stretching to Nanda Devi on clear days.

45 min · Drive

Bhimtal & Sattal Lakes

Seven sacred lakes tucked in the forest, perfect for boating and birdwatching.

1.5 hrs · Drive

Nainital

The beloved hill station with its emerald lake, cafes, and colonial charm.

2 hrs · Drive

Getting Here

Delhi NCR → ~7 hours by car via Moradabad–Ramnagar or Kashipur route
Lucknow → ~8 hours by car via Bareilly–Haldwani route
By Train → Nearest railhead: Kathgodam (KGM). Taxi to Ramgarh (~2 hrs)
