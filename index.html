Forest Walks

Ancient trails wind through Ramgarh's oak and rhododendron forests. Walk at sunrise and find the valley still wrapped in mist.

🦜

Birding

Ramgarh is a birder's paradise — Himalayan laughing thrush, kalij pheasant, and if you're quiet enough, a Monal pheasant.

No room service here. Just you, a fully stocked kitchen, and the pleasure of cooking breakfast while the forest wakes up.

Explore Ramgarh

A Village That Exists
Outside of Time

Malla Ramgarh sits at 1,789 metres in the Kumaon Himalayas — a village of orchards, forests, and forgotten paths. Known as the fruit bowl of Kumaon, it was a favourite retreat of the poet Rabindranath Tagore.

Ramgarh Village

Colonial bungalows, orchards, and the famous Tagore writing cottage — a 10-minute walk away.

10 min · Walk

Mukteshwar Temple

A sacred Shiva temple at 2,286m with views stretching to Nanda Devi on clear days.

45 min · Drive

Bhimtal & Sattal Lakes

Seven sacred lakes tucked in the forest, perfect for boating and birdwatching.

1.5 hrs · Drive

Nainital

The beloved hill station with its emerald lake, cafes, and colonial charm.

2 hrs · Drive

Getting Here

Delhi NCR → ~7 hours by car via Moradabad–Ramnagar or Kashipur route
Lucknow → ~8 hours by car via Bareilly–Haldwani route
By Train → Nearest railhead: Kathgodam (KGM). Taxi to Ramgarh (~2 hrs)

FAQ

Common Questions

Are pets allowed?
Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome at Family Retreat – The Forest Dak Bungalow. Please inform us in advance while booking so we can make appropriate arrangements for your pet's comfort.
How many guests can stay?
The cottage accommodates up to 6 adults across 3 private bedrooms. The entire cottage is yours exclusively — no shared spaces with other guests.
Is the entire cottage private?
Absolutely. Family Retreat is booked as a whole — one group at a time. You get the full cottage: all 3 bedrooms, the kitchen, dining room, TV lounge, and outdoor spaces exclusively for your group.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Standard check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged based on availability — please mention your preference at the time of booking.
Is there a minimum stay?
Yes, the minimum booking period is 2 nights. We recommend at least 3 nights to truly slow down and experience Ramgarh at its best.
How do I confirm my booking?
Fill in the enquiry form or message us on WhatsApp at +91 930 750 9511. We'll check availability, share pricing, and confirm your stay once a deposit is received.
